## Reading the reference

- **Stars** also indicate the spell’s gold cost.
- **T** means combat ticks. Conditional cast times are explained in the effect text.
- Upgrades unlock at **3 XP**. The upgraded column gives the **complete upgraded effect**, including effects that stay the same.
- “Nature attuned”, “Water attuned”, etc. mark effects requiring that domain. In-game these effects use domain colour rather than an extra label.
- **Apply** targets the enemy; **Gain** targets yourself, unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- **Channel:** link up to three matching spells in adjacent slots. Each extra copy adds the listed bonus to every spell in that group.
- **Summon:** the Imp arrives at the end of the tick. A living Imp gains current and maximum Health; a defeated Imp can be summoned again.
- **Ward:** absorbs direct damage, with overflow reaching Health. Damage over time bypasses Ward. Ward does not stack; the higher current or newly granted amount wins.
