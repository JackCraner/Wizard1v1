# Attunement readability review

The card mechanics retain their authored requirements. Presentation now distinguishes normal effects, conditional bonuses and whole-spell requirements.

## Design rules

- A keyword is not a requirement: Agony applies Poison without Nature attunement; Prayer grants Regeneration without Nature attunement.
- Normal effects use neutral text with bold keywords. A domain-colored left border and colored full clause mark an explicit requirement. Labels provide the same information without relying on color.
- Color follows the required domain, not the keyword theme: upgraded Prayer’s Resilience is a Holy condition, and Malison’s stronger Curse is an Affliction condition.
- Whole-spell locks say the spell needs attunement. Partial locks explain that the base effect still works. Cast-speed bonuses preserve their fallback text.
- The two-slot attunement panel shows domain art and card counts. Its priority view shows active/dormant domains, count bars and the oldest-card tie rule.

## Catalogue review

Basic damage, healing and resource builders remain usable across domains. Explicit engine, multiplier and high-impact payoff effects retain their authored gates. Star rarity alone does not impose a requirement; expensive ungated spells remain intentional flexible picks until a balance change is requested.
